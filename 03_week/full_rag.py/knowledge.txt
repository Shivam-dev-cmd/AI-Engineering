A Vector Database is a database designed specifically to store, index, and query high-dimensional numeric arrays called embeddings.  While relational databases match exact values (e.g., WHERE status = 'active'), vector databases measure mathematical proximity—finding items that share semantic meaning.  How Embeddings WorkAI models convert unstructured data (text, images, audio) into vectors—lists of numbers like [0.12, -0.59, 0.88, ..., 0.04] representing concept locations in multi-dimensional space.  In vector space, "cat" and "feline" sit close together, even though they share no common letters.Similarity Metrics (like Cosine Similarity or Euclidean Distance) calculate how close two vectors are.  Key Features of Vector DatabasesApproximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) Search: Checking every single vector for exact matches is too slow at scale. ANN algorithms trade a tiny fraction of accuracy for massive speed gains.  Specialized Indexing:HNSW (Hierarchical Navigable Small World): A multi-layer graph that enables extremely fast sub-millisecond retrieval (best for low-latency, memory-heavy workloads).  DiskANN / IVF: Disk-based indexing that reduces RAM requirements for scaling to millions or billions of vectors.  Hybrid Search & Metadata Filtering: Allows combining semantic similarity search with traditional metadata criteria (e.g., “Find documents related to 'renewable energy' filtered by year >= 2024”).
